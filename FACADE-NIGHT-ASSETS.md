## Запрос генерации

Один и тот же запрос применён отдельно к каждому из четырёх исходных оттенков:

Use case: lighting-weather. Asset type: matched day/night facade selector for an architectural website. Input image 1 is the edit target. Create a photorealistic NIGHT version of this EXACT flat-roofed house image. Change ONLY time of day and lighting: deep blue night sky and cool moonlit forest, welcoming warm 2700K interior light behind the existing glazing, soft up/down light from existing wall lamps, subtle pools of light at existing garden bollards. Retain the ORIGINAL facade material COLOR (do not recolor it), all vertical slats, roof, windows, doors, stairs, terrace, furniture, plants, paving, framing and camera viewpoint. House and facade remain clearly legible at night, not black. Preserve exact composition and edge alignment, full-bleed 16:9 landscape. No added features, lights or objects; no people, text, logos or watermark.
